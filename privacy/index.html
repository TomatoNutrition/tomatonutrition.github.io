Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Aipo is a nutrition-tracking app for the UK, US and Brazil. You photograph a meal, an AI estimates its nutrition, and you can answer short questions to make the estimate more accurate. Aipo gives general wellness estimates, not medical advice.

Who we are

Aipo is run by the Aipo team. We are the "controller" of your personal data, which means we decide how it is used.

Contact us about privacy, or about anything in this policy, at onpowerbr@gmail.com.

Aipo collects only what it needs to estimate your meals and track your day. You don't give us your name, email or phone number to use it.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your data while you use Aipo, and delete it when you delete your account.

  • Delete everything yourself, any time: in the app, go to Profile, then "Delete my account and data". This permanently deletes your guest account, profile, meal records and photo analysis records straight away. It cannot be undone.
  • Inactive accounts: we automatically delete accounts that haven't opened the app or logged a meal for 24 months, together with all their data.
  • Backups: deleted data can stay in encrypted database backups for up to 7 days before it is overwritten.
  • Uninstalling the app removes the data on your phone, but not the data in our database. Delete your account first if you want that gone too. Because you use Aipo as a guest, we can't reconnect you to your old data after an uninstall.

Security. Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored in Supabase's encrypted database. Database rules let each guest account read and change only its own meals and profile. Our AI service key stays on our server and is never placed in the app.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you can see, correct, download or delete your data. Delete it yourself in the app, or email onpowerbr@gmail.com for anything else; we reply within 30 days (15 days in Brazil).

  • UK and EU (UK GDPR / GDPR): access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interests, and withdrawing consent. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) in the UK, or your local data protection authority in the EU.
  • Brazil (LGPD): confirmation that we process your data, access, correction, anonymisation or deletion of unnecessary data, portability, information about who we share it with, and withdrawing consent. You can complain to the ANPD (Autoridade Nacional de Proteção de Dados).
  • United States: depending on your state (for example California, Colorado, Virginia, Washington), you can know, access, correct and delete your data. We do not sell or "share" personal data for targeted advertising. Washington's My Health My Data Act and similar laws cover health data: we collect it only with your consent and you can delete it at any time.

Because you use Aipo as a guest, we may ask you to describe a recent meal you logged (date and foods), so we can find your data without asking for ID.

Children, health and changes

Age. Aipo is for people aged 13 and over. If you live in the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein or Norway, you must be 16 or over. The app checks your age in the first-time questions. We don't set weight-loss targets for anyone under 18. If you think a child has used Aipo, email us and we will delete their data.

Health information. Meal records, weight and goals can say something about your health, so we treat them as sensitive data: collected only with your consent, used only to run Aipo, and never used for advertising.

Not medical advice. Aipo's numbers are estimates for general wellness. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or have a history of disordered eating, talk to a health professional.

Changes. If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before important changes take effect.
